# Coverage Policy Criteria

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to commercial plans only.

---

## LMB-114: Lumbar MRI

**Approve when ALL of the following are met:**
1. Symptom duration of at least 6 weeks, AND
2. Documented trial of conservative therapy (physical therapy, chiropractic, or
   supervised home exercise) for at least 4 weeks, AND
3. No prior lumbar MRI within the preceding 12 months for the same indication.

**Approve immediately, bypassing criteria 1 through 3, if ANY red flag is present:**
- New or progressive neurological deficit (motor weakness, foot drop)
- Bowel or bladder dysfunction of new onset
- Suspected cauda equina syndrome
- History of malignancy with new back pain
- Suspected spinal infection (fever, IV drug use, recent spinal procedure)

**Deny when:** symptom duration under 6 weeks with no red flags, or no documented
conservative therapy.

---

## KNE-207: Knee Arthroscopy

**Approve when ALL of the following are met:**
1. Imaging (MRI or equivalent) documenting a structural abnormality, AND
2. Failed conservative management of at least 6 weeks, including physical therapy, AND
3. Mechanical symptoms documented (locking, catching, or giving way) OR persistent
   effusion.

**Deny when:** no imaging on file, or conservative management not attempted or
documented.

---

## BAR-330: Bariatric Surgery

**Approve when ALL of the following are met:**
1. BMI of 40 or greater, OR BMI of 35 or greater with at least one qualifying
   comorbidity (type 2 diabetes, obstructive sleep apnea, hypertension), AND
2. Documented participation in a supervised weight management program for at least
   6 consecutive months within the past 2 years, AND
3. Psychological evaluation completed within the past 12 months.

**Deny when:** any criterion above is unmet or not documented.

---

## CCT-521: Cardiac CT Angiography

**Approve when ANY of the following are met:**
1. Acute or stable chest pain with intermediate pretest probability of coronary
   artery disease, AND an equivocal or non-diagnostic stress test, OR
2. Suspected anomalous coronary artery, OR
3. Pre-operative evaluation before non-coronary cardiac surgery.

**Deny when:** requested for routine surveillance in an asymptomatic patient with
known coronary artery disease, or as a screening test in an asymptomatic patient
with no risk stratification documented.

---

## Cross-cutting rules

- If required documentation is absent rather than negative, the correct outcome is
  **needs-info**, not deny. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Any request where the clinical note suggests an urgent or emergent condition must
  be flagged for immediate clinical review regardless of the procedure-level outcome.
- Every determination must cite the specific policy ID and the numbered criterion
  that drove the outcome.
