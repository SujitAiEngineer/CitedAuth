Stop and assess where the build actually is. Look at the current state of the
code, not at what we intended.

Anything I want to add:

$ARGUMENTS

Append to `scratch/checkpoints.md` with a timestamp, and print it.

Four sections. One to three bullets each. No padding, no encouragement.

## Works
What is actually running and verified end to end right now. If I have not seen
it produce output, it does not go here.

## Faked
Every stub, hardcoded value, and happy path assumption currently in the code.
Be exhaustive. This is the list I read out loud so nothing looks like a claim
I did not make.

## Next
The single next step, and the one after it. Not a backlog.

## Got wrong
What I assumed earlier that turned out to be false, and what it cost.
If genuinely nothing, say "nothing yet" rather than manufacturing something.

Be blunt. An accurate bad status is worth more to me than a flattering one.
